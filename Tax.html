How to Use the Tax Calculator

Step 1: Enter Your Income
Enter your total monthly income in the "Enter Your Income" box.
Step 2: Add Mandatory Contributions
Enter your SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG contributions (if applicable).
Step 3: Non-Taxable Benefits
Enter any non-taxable benefits (e.g., 13th Month pay, Rice Subsidy, etc.).
Step 4: Calculate Tax
Click the "CALCULATE" button to see your tax calculation, tax owed, and take-home pay.
Step 5: View Results
The graph will show your income, tax owed, and take-home pay for easy visualization.
